About

A little about me

I'm an associate researcher in the Elahi Lab at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, where I run biostatistical analyses across clinical, proteomic, genomic, transcriptomic, and imaging data. What gets me excited is the intersection of medicine, engineering, and technology, and the chance to bring all three together to solve real problems in human health.

Before Mount Sinai, I was at MIT, where I earned my M.Eng. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science in 2024 with a focus on AI and machine learning. My master's thesis and many of my earlier projects used vision-language models to build lightweight, flexible AI representations of real-world environments, helping machines make sense of the spaces around them.

When I'm not working, you'll probably find me dancing salsa or out with my camera. Some of my favorite shots are in the photography section below.
